Lockheed Martin Corporation (NYSE: LMT) is a leading American aerospace and defense company headquartered in Bethesda, Maryland. Established in 1995 through the merger of Lockheed Corporation and Martin Marietta, the company specializes in the research, design, development, manufacture, integration, and sustainment of advanced technology systems, products, and services. - **Aeronautics**: Development and production of combat and air mobility aircraft, unmanned air vehicles, and related technologies.

- **Missiles and Fire Control (MFC)**: Provision of air and missile defense systems, tactical missiles, precision strike weapons, and fire control systems.

- **Rotary and Mission Systems (RMS)**: Manufacture of military and commercial helicopters, surface ships, radar systems, and cyber solutions.

- **Space**: Development of satellites, space transportation systems, and strategic missile defense systems. ([lockheedmartin.com](https://www.lockheedmartin.com/en-us/who-we-are.html?utm_source=openai))

A significant portion of Lockheed Martin's business is with the U.S. Department of Defense and other federal agencies, including the Department of Energy and NASA.
