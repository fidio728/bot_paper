Alabama Power Company, a subsidiary of Southern Company, is an investor-owned electric utility headquartered in Birmingham, Alabama. Established in 1906, it provides electricity to approximately 1.4 million customers across the southern two-thirds of Alabama, covering a service area of about 44,500 square miles.
