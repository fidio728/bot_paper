Teradyne Inc. (TER) is a leading supplier of automated test equipment (ATE) used to test semiconductors, wireless products, data storage, and complex electronic systems. The company operates through several segments:

- **Semiconductor Test**: Provides test solutions for semiconductor devices, including system-on-chip (SoC) and memory devices.

- **System Test**: Offers test systems for storage, defense, and aerospace applications.

- **Wireless Test**: Delivers wireless test solutions for devices such as smartphones and tablets.

- **Industrial Automation**: Through its Universal Robots and MiR (Mobile Industrial Robots) subsidiaries, Teradyne provides collaborative and autonomous robots for industrial applications.

Founded in 1960 and headquartered in North Reading, Massachusetts, Teradyne serves a global customer base, including major semiconductor manufacturers and electronics companies.
