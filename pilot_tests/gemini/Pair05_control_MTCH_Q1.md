Match Group, Inc. (MTCH) is a prominent internet and technology company that operates a large portfolio of online dating services. Headquartered in Dallas, Texas, the company owns and manages well-known dating platforms such as Tinder, Match.com, Meetic, OkCupid, Hinge, and Plenty of Fish, among others.

Match Group's core business revolves around providing digital technologies that facilitate meaningful connections between people. They operate through various portfolio companies, each managing a global set of online dating and relationship brands. The company's services are available in over 40 languages and are used worldwide, catering to diverse user preferences and relationship goals. Tinder is identified as the company's flagship brand and a significant contributor to its revenue.

The company's strategy involves a multi-phase approach focused on product development and user outcomes, aiming to enhance user experience and safety. Match Group also emphasizes trust and safety, implementing features like facial verification to confirm user identities. Their business model primarily generates revenue from user fees, with a smaller portion coming from advertising.
