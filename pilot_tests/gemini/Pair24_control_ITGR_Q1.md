INTEGER HOLDINGS CORP (ITGR) is a leading global contract development and manufacturing organization (CDMO) for medical devices. The company specializes in serving the cardiac rhythm management, neuromodulation, and cardio and vascular markets. As a strategic partner, Integer works with medical device companies and original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) to advance their goals through engineering and manufacturing expertise.

They provide a wide range of products, from finished device systems and Class III Medical Devices to hundreds of components, including guidewires, introducer sheaths, and implants for various procedures.
