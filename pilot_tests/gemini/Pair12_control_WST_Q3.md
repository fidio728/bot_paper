West Pharmaceutical Services, Inc. (WST) is a global leader in providing innovative packaging components and delivery systems for injectable drugs and healthcare products. The company is headquartered in Exton, Pennsylvania, and has a significant global presence with operations across North and South America, Europe, Asia, and Australia.

Investor relations information for West Pharmaceutical Services can be found on their official investor relations website. Key highlights from their investor relations materials include:

**Financial Performance and Outlook:**
*   **2025 Performance:** The company reported record sales for 2025, with net sales surpassing $3 billion and organic growth of 4%. Adjusted earnings per share (EPS) grew by 8%, and free cash flow saw a substantial 70% increase. 2025 was a year of transition, with strong momentum in both GLP-1 and core businesses exceeding expectations. They successfully managed destocking in High-Value Components and transitioned the SmartDose business to AbbVie.
*   **2026 Outlook:** West Pharmaceutical Services forecasts 5% to 7% organic revenue growth for 2026, with the majority of this growth expected from their non-GLP-1 business, particularly High-Value Products (HVP). EPS is projected to grow in the double digits. GLP-1 sales, which grew over 50% in 2025, are conservatively guided at approximately 10% for 2026, though there is potential for upside. The company expects margin expansion in 2026 due to a favorable product mix and operational improvements. The sale of the SmartDose business is anticipated to close mid-2026.

**Business Model and Market Position:**
*   West Pharmaceutical Services holds a significant market share, estimated between 70%-75%, with a strong competitive advantage, especially in biologics. This position ensures long-term customer retention.
*   The company's business model centers on close partnerships with pharmaceutical and biotechnology firms worldwide. West focuses on developing and manufacturing containment and delivery solutions, allowing client companies to concentrate on drug formulation and therapeutic efficacy. They have a robust research and development pipeline committed to innovation in drug delivery for enhanced safety and efficacy.
*   The "Annex 1 Opportunity" is identified as a multiyear growth driver, with significant potential still untapped. Over 700 projects have been initiated, contributing to future revenues.

**Recent Developments:**
*   In March 2026, West announced an expansion of their Dublin facility to support high-volume injectable therapies, including those for diabetes and obesity.
*   Eric M. Green announced his plans to retire as President, CEO, and Chair of the Board in March 2026.

West Pharmaceutical Services is listed on the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE: WST) and is part of the Standard & Poor's 500 index.
