What does SANGOMA TECHNOLOGIES CORP (SANG) do?

---

# gemini response

Sangoma Technologies Corp. (SANG) is a Canadian company that specializes in providing Communications as a Service (CaaS) solutions, Voice over IP (VoIP) hardware, and software for businesses. Founded in 1984, Sangoma offers a comprehensive suite of communication tools designed to enhance business connectivity, productivity, and collaboration.

The company's offerings include:

*   **Unified Communications as a Service (UCaaS):** Sangoma provides cloud-based and hybrid solutions that integrate voice, video, messaging, and call center capabilities into a single platform. These solutions are designed for businesses of all sizes, from small to medium businesses (SMBs) to enterprises.
*   **Contact Center as a Service (CCaaS):** Sangoma CX is their cloud-native contact center suite that helps businesses manage customer interactions across multiple channels.
*   **VoIP Hardware and Software:** Sangoma develops and distributes a range of products such as telephony cards, gateways, session border controllers (SBCs), and IP desk phones. They are also a primary developer and sponsor of open-source projects like Asterisk and FreePBX, which are widely used in the telecommunications industry.
*   **Managed Services:** The company offers managed connectivity, network, and security services, including SD-WAN, VPN, and firewall solutions.
*   **Productivity Apps:** Sangoma also provides various productivity applications aimed at improving business operations.

Sangoma operates with a business model focused on high-margin recurring revenue from its platforms and aims to offer a single-vendor framework for unified voice, video, networking, and security.
