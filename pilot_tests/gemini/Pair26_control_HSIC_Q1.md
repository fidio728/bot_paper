# you asked

What does HENRY SCHEIN INC (HSIC) do?

---

# gemini response

Henry Schein, Inc. (HSIC) is a global provider of health care products and services, primarily serving office-based dental and medical practitioners. The company is recognized as the world's largest distributor of health care solutions to these professionals.

Henry Schein operates through several segments:
*   **Global Distribution and Value-Added Services:** This segment distributes a wide array of products, including infection-control items, dental instruments, pharmaceuticals, vaccines, surgical products, diagnostic tests, dental equipment, and personal protective equipment (PPE). They also offer services such as equipment repair, financial services, and continuing education for practitioners.
*   **Global Specialty Products:** This segment focuses on the manufacturing, marketing, and sales of dental implant and biomaterial products, as well as endodontic, orthodontic, and orthopedic products.
*   **Global Technology:** This segment is involved in the development and distribution of practice management software, e-services, and other technology products for healthcare providers. Henry Schein One, a division within this segment, delivers integrated dental technology to improve practice management and patient communication.

The company offers over 300,000 branded and private-brand products through its distribution network. Henry Schein also provides business, clinical, technology, and supply chain solutions to enhance the efficiency of practices. They serve over 1 million customers globally, including dental laboratories, government and institutional health care clinics, and alternate care sites.
