What does SEABOARD CORP (SEB) do?

---

# gemini response

Seaboard Corporation (SEB) is a diverse, multinational agribusiness and transportation company with integrated operations across several industries. The company operates globally through various segments, including Pork, Commodity Trading and Milling (CT&M), Marine, Liquid Fuels, Power, and Turkey.

In the United States, Seaboard is primarily involved in pork production and processing, as well as ocean transportation. Their pork division, Seaboard Foods, is a leading pork producer and processor, raising and processing millions of hogs annually, and selling pork products to further processors, food service operators, grocery stores, and distributors, both domestically and internationally. Seaboard Marine provides containerized shipping services between the United States, the Caribbean, and Central and South America.

Internationally, Seaboard engages in commodity merchandising, grain processing, sugar production, and electric power generation. The CT&M segment sources, transports, and markets various commodities like wheat, corn, and soybeans, and produces products such as flour and animal feed. Seaboard also has interests in sugar and alcohol production, as well as a turkey operation through its stake in Butterball, LLC. Additionally, their Power segment operates as an independent power producer in the Dominican Republic. The company also produces biodiesel fuel.
