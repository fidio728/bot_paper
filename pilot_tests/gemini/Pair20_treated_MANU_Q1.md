MANCHESTER UNITED PLC (MANU) is a global sports and entertainment company that operates Manchester United Football Club, one of the world's most recognized professional football franchises. The company's core business involves the management, promotion, and monetization of the Manchester United brand through its football operations and worldwide commercial activities.

MANU generates revenue from three primary streams:
*   **Commercial:** This segment includes income from sponsorships, retail sales, merchandising, apparel, and product licensing. The company partners with international and regional companies for sponsorship deals and sells branded merchandise through retail centers, e-commerce platforms, and wholesale channels.
*   **Broadcasting:** Revenue in this category comes from domestic and international media rights associated with the club's participation in various football leagues and cup competitions, such as the Premier League and UEFA competitions.
*   **Matchday:** This revenue stream is generated from ticket sales, hospitality services, and concessions at the club's stadium on match days.

The company's operations encompass both men's and women's first teams, as well as youth academy development. MANU also leverages its brand through proprietary media content platforms and global retail and e-commerce channels. Key assets include Old Trafford Stadium and the Aon Training Complex.
