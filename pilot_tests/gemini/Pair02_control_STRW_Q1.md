What does STRAWBERRY FIELDS REIT INC (STRW) do?

# gemini response

Strawberry Fields REIT Inc. (STRW) is a real estate investment trust (REIT) that focuses on acquiring, owning, and leasing skilled nursing facilities and other healthcare-related properties. The company primarily leases these facilities to third-party operators under long-term triple-net lease agreements.

STRW's portfolio consists of skilled nursing facilities (SNFs), assisted living facilities (ALFs), and long-term acute care hospitals (LTACHs). As of recent reports, the company owns and leases a substantial number of these facilities, with figures ranging from 130 to over 140 properties and more than 14,500 to over 15,500 licensed beds across multiple states, primarily in the Midwest and South.

The company's business model involves acquiring new senior living facilities and managing its existing portfolio to improve efficiency and provide high-quality care. STRW often focuses on smaller, off-market transactions that larger REITs may not pursue. This strategy has contributed to consistent growth in their Adjusted Funds From Operations (AFFO) and Adjusted EBITDA.
