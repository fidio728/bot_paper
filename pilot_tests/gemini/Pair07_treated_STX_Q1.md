What does SEAGATE TECHNOLOGY HOLDINGS (STX) do?

---

# gemini response

Seagate Technology Holdings (STX) is a company that specializes in developing, producing, and distributing data storage technology and infrastructure solutions. Their product offerings include a wide range of storage devices such as hard disk drives (HDDs), solid-state drives (SSDs), and hybrid drives. They also provide storage subsystems, computing solutions, and mass capacity storage products for enterprise use.

Seagate's solutions cater to various needs, from consumer devices like computers, laptops, and gaming consoles to enterprise-level data centers and cloud storage infrastructure. They offer external storage solutions under brands like LaCie, Maxtor, Backup Plus, and Expansion.

The company has been a significant player in the data storage market since its founding in 1979, notably developing the first 5.25-inch hard disk drive. In recent times, Seagate has been noted for its advancements in Heat-Assisted Magnetic Recording (HAMR) technology, which is seen as a key driver for its growth in the AI infrastructure sector.
