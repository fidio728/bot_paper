What does MADISON SQUARE GARDEN SPORTS (MSGS) do?

# gemini response

Madison Square Garden Sports Corp. (MSGS) is a prominent sports holding company based in New York City. The company's primary focus is on managing professional sports teams, most notably the National Basketball Association's New York Knicks and the National Hockey League's New York Rangers.

In addition to these flagship teams, MSGS also operates their development league affiliates: the Westchester Knicks (NBAGL) and the Hartford Wolf Pack (AHL). The company also runs the MSG Training Center, a performance center for its sports teams.

MSGS is committed to community engagement, leveraging its brands to support social impact initiatives, including food donations, educational resources for schools, and fundraising for non-profits. They also run grassroots programs like Junior Knicks and Junior Rangers to create inclusive opportunities for children in basketball and hockey.

The company underwent restructuring, with its entertainment division being spun off as Madison Square Garden Entertainment in April 2020, while the remaining sports-focused entity was renamed Madison Square Garden Sports Corp.
