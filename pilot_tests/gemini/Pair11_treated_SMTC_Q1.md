# you asked

What does SEMTECH CORP (SMTC) do?

---

# gemini response

Semtech Corporation (SMTC) is a global provider of high-performance semiconductors, Internet of Things (IoT) systems, and cloud connectivity services. The company develops, manufactures, and markets a diverse range of products for consumer, enterprise computing, communications, and industrial markets.

Semtech's offerings include:
*   **Semiconductors:** These are high-performance, analog, and mixed-signal integrated circuits. They power applications in areas such as AI data center networking, IoT connectivity, and intelligent connected devices. Their signal integrity products are used in data communications, video transport, enterprise networks, and optical transceivers.
*   **IoT Systems and Connectivity:** Semtech is known for its LoRa Technology, a long-range, low-power wireless networking initiative for the Internet of Things. They also provide modules, gateways, routers, and cloud-based services for IoT applications.
*   **Other Products:** These include protection devices to safeguard electronic systems from voltage spikes, radio frequency products for industrial and medical applications, and specialized sensing products. They also offer switching voltage regulators and wireless charging solutions.

The company operates in several segments, including Signal Integrity, Analog Mixed Signal and Wireless, and IoT Systems and Connectivity. Semtech was founded in 1960 and is headquartered in Camarillo, California.
