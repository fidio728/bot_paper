What does NOBILITY HOMES INC (NOBH) do?

---

# gemini response

Nobility Homes, Inc. (NOBH) designs, manufactures, and sells manufactured and modular homes. The company operates through its own retail sales centers in Florida and also sells homes on a wholesale basis to independent dealers and manufactured home communities.

Founded in 1967 and headquartered in Ocala, Florida, Nobility Homes offers approximately 100 different models under trade names such as Kingswood, Richwood, Tropic Isle, Regency Manor, and Tropic Manor. The homes range in size from 464 to 2,800 square feet, with one to five bedrooms.

Through its subsidiary, Prestige Home Centers, Nobility Homes also handles financing and insurance for home buyers, offering a "one-stop shopping" experience. The company emphasizes affordable housing and has provided homes for nearly 56,000 families since its inception.
