Old Dominion Freight Line (ODFL) is a U.S.-based transportation and logistics company that specializes in Less Than Truckload (LTL) freight services. They provide regional, inter-regional, and national LTL shipping solutions across North America, serving manufacturers, retailers, distributors, and other businesses.

In addition to their core LTL services, ODFL also offers:
*   **Expedited transportation:** For time-sensitive shipments.
*   **Logistics services:** Including supply chain consulting to help clients optimize their logistics.
*   **Household moving services:**
*   **Container drayage and truckload brokerage:**
*   **Global shipping:** Through strategic partnerships, offering Full Container Load (FCL) and Less-than-Container Load (LCL) services to various international destinations.

Founded in 1934, ODFL has grown from a single-truck operation into one of the largest LTL carriers in the United States. The company emphasizes premium service, focusing on on-time delivery and careful handling of freight, which differentiates them from competitors who may focus primarily on price. ODFL operates an extensive network of service centers across the U.S., Canada, and Mexico, supported by a large fleet of tractors and trailers. They are known for their high service quality, industry-leading on-time delivery rates, and low claims ratio.
