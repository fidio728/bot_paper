# you asked

What does TERADYNE INC (TER) do?

---

# gemini response

Teradyne, Inc. (TER) designs, develops, and manufactures automated test equipment (ATE) and advanced robotics systems. The company is headquartered in North Reading, Massachusetts.

Teradyne operates through two main segments: Semiconductor Test and Robotics. The Semiconductor Test segment provides products and services for testing semiconductor devices at various stages, including wafer level, device package testing, and system-level testing. These semiconductors are used in a wide range of applications such as automotive, industrial, communications, consumer electronics, smartphones, cloud computing, and gaming. They are essential for testing computer chips before they are used in devices like phones, data centers, or AI hardware.

The Robotics segment offers collaborative robotic arms and autonomous mobile robots designed for manufacturing, logistics, and industrial customers. Teradyne also provides product test instruments for defense/aerospace, circuit-board testing, wireless systems, and silicon photonics testing. The company has a broad customer base including major technology firms like Samsung, Qualcomm, Intel, Analog Devices, Texas Instruments, and IBM.
