What does LANDS' END INC (LE) do?

# gemini response

Lands' End, Inc. is a digital retailer that offers a variety of products including apparel, swimwear, outerwear, accessories, footwear, home products, and uniforms. The company sells its products online through its website, landsend.com, as well as through third-party distribution channels and its own company-operated stores. Lands' End also provides products to businesses and schools for their employees and students through its Outfitters distribution channel.

The company began in 1963 as a mail-order yachting supply company in Chicago. It has since expanded into general clothing and home furnishings and is now headquartered in Dodgeville, Wisconsin. In 2026, Lands' End formed a joint venture with WHP Global to accelerate global brand expansion. Lands' End maintains operational control of its direct-to-consumer and business-to-business operations, while WHP Global leverages its brand-management platform to expand the brand internationally and into new categories.
