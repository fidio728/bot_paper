What does OSHKOSH CORP (OSK) do?

# gemini response

Oshkosh Corporation (OSK) is a global industrial company that designs, manufactures, and distributes specialty vehicles and heavy equipment. Founded in 1917, the company is headquartered in Oshkosh, Wisconsin, and operates through four primary business segments:

*   **Access Equipment:** This segment produces aerial work platforms and telehandlers used in construction, maintenance, and industrial applications. Brands in this segment include JLG and SkyTrak.
*   **Defense:** Oshkosh designs and manufactures tactical wheeled vehicles and mobility systems for the U.S. Department of Defense and international military customers. Key products include the Joint Light Tactical Vehicle (JLTV) and the Heavy Expanded Mobility Tactical Truck (HEMTT). This segment's performance is often linked to U.S. government contracts.
*   **Fire & Emergency:** This division manufactures fire apparatus and emergency vehicles, including pumpers, aerials, tankers, ambulances, and police vehicles. Major brands include Pierce Manufacturing.
*   **Commercial:** This segment focuses on specialized vehicles such as refuse collection vehicles, concrete mixers, and other commercial trucks.

Oshkosh Corporation also has a significant presence in airport fire apparatus and aviation ground support equipment. The company owns multiple brands, each with its own specialty, and has a global presence with facilities in numerous countries.
