What does TFI INTERNATIONAL INC (TFII) do?

# gemini response

TFI International Inc. (TFII) is a Canadian transportation and logistics company that operates primarily in Canada, the United States, and Mexico. The company offers a wide range of services through four main business segments: Less-Than-Truckload (LTL), Package and Courier, Logistics, and Truckload.

Key aspects of TFI International's business include:
*   **Less-Than-Truckload (LTL):** This segment involves the pickup, consolidation, transport, and delivery of smaller freight loads from multiple customers. TFI International has Canada's largest LTL business.
*   **Package and Courier:** This segment focuses on parcel delivery and last-mile logistics.
*   **Logistics:** TFI International's logistics segment provides asset-light services such as brokerage, freight forwarding, and transportation management. It also includes specialized services like chemical transportation and medical logistics.
*   **Truckload:** This segment handles full truckloads of goods transported directly from the customer to the destination, often using specialized equipment. TFI International has one of the largest trucking fleets in North America. The company is also experiencing growth in its specialized truckload services for data center and power grid customers.

TFI International has grown significantly through strategic acquisitions, such as the purchase of UPS Freight in 2021, which was renamed TForce Freight. The company's business model relies on integrating acquired companies and optimizing their performance.
