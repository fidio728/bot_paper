# gemini response

BioMarin Pharmaceutical Inc. (BMRN) is a global biotechnology company that focuses on developing and commercializing therapies for rare genetic diseases and medical conditions. Founded in 1997, the company has a history of innovation and has brought eight commercial therapies to market, with a strong pipeline of clinical and preclinical candidates.

BioMarin's core business and research involve enzyme replacement therapies (ERTs). They were the first company to offer therapeutics for mucopolysaccharidosis type I (MPS I) and phenylketonuria (PKU). Their product portfolio includes treatments for conditions such as achondroplasia, severe hemophilia A, and various rare inherited and lysosomal storage diseases. The company utilizes a distinctive approach to drug discovery, aiming to harness genetic science to create category-defining medicines that significantly impact patients' lives.
