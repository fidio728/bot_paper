What does WEST PHARMACEUTICAL SVSC INC (WST) do?

---

# gemini response

West Pharmaceutical Services, Inc. (WST) is a global manufacturer specializing in packaging components and delivery systems for injectable drugs and healthcare products. The company plays a crucial role in the pharmaceutical and biotechnology supply chain, providing essential products that ensure the safe and effective containment and delivery of medicines.

West's offerings include:
*   **Packaging Components:** These are primarily elastomer-based items such as stoppers, seals, and plungers that come into direct contact with the drug.
*   **Containment and Delivery Systems:** This includes a range of solutions like pre-fillable syringe systems, auto-injectors, pen injectors, and on-body delivery technologies.
*   **Contract Manufacturing:** West also manufactures medical devices and complex devices for pharmaceutical, diagnostic, and medical device customers based on their intellectual property.

The company serves both established and emerging drug developers, working to improve patient health by ensuring the quality and reliability of injectable therapies. West operates through two main business segments: proprietary products and contract-manufactured products.
