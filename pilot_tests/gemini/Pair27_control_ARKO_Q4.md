The management team at ARKO Corp. (ARKO) is led by Arie Kotler, who serves as Chairman and Chief Executive Officer. Kotler has held these roles since December 2020, following the merger with Haymaker Acquisition Corp. II. He also serves as President and has been CEO and President of GPM Investments, LLC since 2011 and 2015, respectively.

Other key members of the leadership team include:

*   **Maury Bricks:** General Counsel and Secretary. He has held this position at ARKO Corp. since December 2020 and at GPM since January 2013.
*   **Irit Aviram:** Executive Vice President, Office of the Chairman and CEO.
*   **Efrat Hybloom-Klein:** Executive Vice President, Office of the Chairman and CEO.
*   **Eyal Nuchamovitz:** Executive Vice President, Business Development and M&A.
*   **Jordan Mann:** Chief Financial Officer. He was promoted to interim CFO in October 2025 and has served as Senior Vice President, Corporate Strategy, Capital Markets, and Investor Relations since May 2023.
*   **Galagher Jeff:** Appointed Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer effective December 1, 2025. Prior to this, he was the CFO of Murphy USA, Inc.
*   **Ross Parman:** Vice President of Investor Relations and Government Affairs. He was appointed in December 2021.

The management team has an average tenure of approximately 4.3 years.
