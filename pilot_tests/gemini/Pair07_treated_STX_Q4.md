SEAGATE TECHNOLOGY HOLDINGS (STX) management team and leadership

# gemini response

Dave Mosley is the Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of Seagate Technology Holdings plc. He has held the CEO position since October 2017 and also serves on the company's Board of Directors, to which he was first elected in July 2017. In July 2025, Mosley was elected to the additional role of Board Chair, succeeding Michael R. Cannon.

The Seagate leadership team is described as seasoned and experienced, with an average tenure of 7.3 years. Other key executives include:

*   **Gianluca Romano:** Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer
*   **Dr. John Morris:** Executive Vice President and Chief Technology Officer
*   **B.S. Teh:** Executive Vice President and Chief Commercial Officer
*   **K.F. Chong:** Executive Vice President of Global Operations
*   **Jim Lee:** Executive Vice President, Chief Legal Officer, and Corporate Secretary
*   **Patricia Frost:** Executive Vice President and Chief People and Places Officer

Michael R. Cannon, the former Chairman, will continue to serve as the Lead Independent Director.
