What does ALABAMA POWER CO (SO1) do?

# gemini response

Alabama Power Company is an electric utility that provides electricity to approximately 1.5 million residential, commercial, and industrial customers across the southern two-thirds of Alabama. Founded in 1906, it is an investor-owned subsidiary of Southern Company.

Beyond supplying reliable electricity, Alabama Power is involved in strengthening communities through infrastructure investment, innovation, and sustainability initiatives. The company also plays a role in economic development by providing site selection, workforce analysis, and project management services to businesses considering expansion or relocation in Alabama. They have been committed to supporting economic growth in the state for over a century and were the first electric utility in the U.S. to establish an Economic Development Department.

Alabama Power operates a diverse generation portfolio and manages an extensive transmission and distribution network. Their services also extend to helping businesses improve energy efficiency, offering incentives, and providing customized products and services to cut costs and increase productivity. Additionally, the company is involved in managing lakes and shorelines, preserving natural spaces, and engaging in environmental stewardship to protect the state's land and water. They also previously operated appliance stores.
