# gemini response

Trustmark Corporation (TRMK) is a diversified financial services company that provides banking and wealth management solutions. It operates primarily through its subsidiary, Trustmark National Bank, offering a range of services to individuals and businesses across Alabama, Florida, Georgia, Mississippi, Tennessee, and Texas.

The company's services include:
*   **General Banking:** This segment encompasses traditional banking products and services, such as checking and savings accounts, money market accounts, certificates of deposit, loans (commercial, industrial, real estate, and construction), and treasury management services.
*   **Wealth Management:** This segment offers integrated financial services, including money management, full-service brokerage, financial planning, personal and institutional trust services, retirement plan services, and estate administration.
*   **Mortgage Banking:** Trustmark provides mortgage services, including construction financing, production of conventional and government-insured mortgages, secondary marketing, and mortgage servicing.

Trustmark Corporation was founded in 1889 and is headquartered in Jackson, Mississippi.
