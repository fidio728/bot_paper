**ARKO Corp. (ARKO) Investor Relations**

ARKO Corp. is a Fortune 500 company that owns 100% of GPM Investments, LLC, making it one of the largest operators of convenience stores and fuel wholesalers in the United States. The company is headquartered in Richmond, Virginia.

**Key Information for Investors:**

*   **Company Overview:** ARKO operates in four segments: retail (fuel and merchandise sales), wholesale (fuel supply to third-party dealers), GPM Petroleum (fuel supply to ARKO's own retail and wholesale sites), and fleet fueling (cardlock locations and fuel cards).
*   **Stock Information:** The company's stock is traded on the Nasdaq under the symbol ARKO.
*   **Recent News and Events:**
    *   ARKO has been highlighting its "Fueling America's Future" campaign, offering significant savings on fuel to customers.
    *   The company launched a new fas REWARDS® mobile app to enhance customer savings and loyalty.
    *   ARKO has been actively involved in an operational transformation, including dealerizing less strategic retail locations and focusing on core assets.
    *   ARKO Petroleum (APC), a subsidiary of ARKO Corp., recently had an IPO and reported its Q4 and full-year 2025 financial results.
*   **Financial Performance:**
    *   For Q4 2025, ARKO reported net income of $1.9 million, with full-year net income of $22.7 million, an increase of 9.1% year-over-year.
    *   Adjusted EBITDA was $65.7 million for Q4 2025 and $248.7 million for the full year 2025.
    *   The company completed the ARKO Petroleum IPO, raising approximately $184 million which was used to reduce debt.
*   **Growth and Strategy:** ARKO operates over 3,500 locations across more than 30 states and has completed numerous acquisitions since 2013. The company sees significant growth opportunities in the fragmented convenience store industry.
