# you asked

MACY'S INC (M) management team and leadership

---

# gemini response

The current Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of Macy's, Inc. is Tony Spring, who assumed the CEO role in February 2024 and became chairman in April 2024. He previously served as president of Macy's, Inc. and as chairman and CEO of Bloomingdale's.

The leadership team also includes:
*   **Tom Edwards:** Chief Operating Officer and Chief Financial Officer, effective June 22, 2025.
*   **Barbie Cameron:** Chief Stores Officer, appointed in February 2025.
*   **Keith Credendino:** Chief Information Officer, appointed in August 2024.
*   **Nata Dvir:** Chief Merchandising Officer, appointed in February 2021.
*   **Danielle Kirgan:** Executive Vice President, Chief Human Resources and Corporate Affairs Officer.
*   **Max Magni:** Chief Customer and Digital Officer, appointed in August 2023.
*   **Sharon Otterman:** Chief Marketing Officer.
*   **Tracy Preston:** Chief Legal Officer and Corporate Secretary, joined in January 2024.
*   **Olivier Bron:** CEO of Bloomingdale's.
*   **Maly Bernstein:** CEO of Bluemercury, will report to Olivier Bron.

Prior to these recent appointments, Jeff Gennette served as Chairman and CEO. Adrian Mitchell was the COO and CFO and departed in June 2025.
