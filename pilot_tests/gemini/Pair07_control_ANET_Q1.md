What does ARISTA NETWORKS INC (ANET) do?

---

# gemini response

Arista Networks Inc. (ANET) is a computer networking company that designs, develops, and sells cloud networking solutions. These solutions primarily consist of high-performance Ethernet switches, routers, and other networking hardware, all running on their proprietary Linux-based network operating system called Extensible Operating System (EOS).

The company focuses on delivering software-defined networking (SDN) for large data centers, cloud computing environments, high-performance computing, and high-frequency trading. Arista's products are known for their low latency and are used by major tech players like Microsoft and Meta. The company is experiencing strong demand driven by artificial intelligence (AI) and the growth of cloud computing.
