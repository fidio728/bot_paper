What does ADOBE INC (ADBE) do?

# gemini response

Adobe Inc. is a multinational computer software company that provides a wide range of programs for creating, publishing, and managing digital content. The company operates through three main segments: Digital Media, Digital Experience, and Publishing and Advertising.

The **Digital Media** segment offers tools and solutions for individuals and businesses to create, publish, promote, and monetize their content. This includes its flagship Creative Cloud subscription service, which provides access to creative products like Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro, and InDesign. This segment also encompasses Adobe Document Cloud, which offers unified cloud-based document services such as Adobe Acrobat.

The **Digital Experience** segment provides an integrated platform and applications for businesses to create, manage, execute, measure, monetize, and optimize customer experiences across multiple channels. This includes Adobe Experience Cloud, which encompasses marketing, analytics, and advertising solutions.

The **Publishing and Advertising** segment includes legacy products and services such as e-learning solutions, technical document publishing, web conferencing, and web application development.

Adobe's products are essential for professionals in graphic design, video editing, web development, photography, and more, making it a key player in the advancement of digital media. The company is also increasingly integrating AI and generative technologies into its offerings, aiming to empower human creators and expand its capabilities in content creation and digital marketing.
