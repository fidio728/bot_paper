What does CAMPING WORLD HOLDINGS INC (CWH) do?

---

# gemini response

Camping World Holdings, Inc. (CWH) is a major American retailer that specializes in recreational vehicles (RVs), RV parts, and RV services. The company also sells camping supplies and outdoor lifestyle products.

Camping World operates a large network of dealerships across the United States, offering sales of new and used RVs, as well as financing, maintenance, repair, and warranty services. In addition to its physical locations, Camping World sells RV parts, accessories, and gear through its e-commerce platform and phone orders.

The company's offerings extend beyond RVs to include outdoor equipment, apparel, and accessories. Camping World also provides insurance and financing solutions for RVs, aiming to offer a comprehensive ownership experience. Furthermore, they have a program called Good Sam Services and Plans, which includes services like extended vehicle service contracts, roadside assistance, and insurance geared towards RV and travel lifestyles.

Founded in 1966, Camping World has grown significantly and is now considered the world's largest retailer of RVs and related products and services. The company is headquartered in Lincolnshire, Illinois.
