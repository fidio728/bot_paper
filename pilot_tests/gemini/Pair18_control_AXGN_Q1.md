# you asked

What does AXOGEN INC (AXGN) do?

---

# gemini response

Axogen Inc. (AXGN) is a company that specializes in the development and commercialization of technologies for peripheral nerve regeneration and repair. They provide innovative, clinically proven, and economically effective solutions for surgeons and healthcare providers to help patients restore peripheral nerve function and improve their quality of life.

The company's products are designed to address peripheral nerve damage or discontinuity, which can result from traumatic injuries or surgical procedures. These issues can lead to loss of muscle or organ function, sensory feeling, or chronic pain. Axogen's product portfolio includes items like Avance® Nerve Graft (a human nerve allograft) and AxoGuard® Nerve Connector and Nerve Protector (made from porcine submucosa extracellular matrix). These products aim to help patients regain abilities such as touch, movement, and feeling.

Axogen is considered a pioneer in the regenerative medicine market for peripheral nerve repair. As of 2019, they were the only company with a commercially available allograft approved by the FDA for clinical use. Their approach allows surgeons to repair injured nerves without the need for additional surgical sites that are typically required for traditional nerve autograft techniques, thus avoiding associated complications and morbidities.
