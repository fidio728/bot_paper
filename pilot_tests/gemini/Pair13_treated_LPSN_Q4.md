# gemini response

John Sabino is the Chief Executive Officer of LivePerson, Inc. (LPSN) and has held this position since January 2024. Before joining LivePerson, Sabino served as Chief Customer Officer at VMware and Splunk, and as Chief Operating Officer at GE Digital. John Collins holds the roles of Chief Financial Officer and Chief Operating Officer at LivePerson, having previously served as Interim CEO.

The executive leadership team also includes:
*   **Barbara Dawson:** Chief People Officer
*   **Monica Greenberg:** Chief Legal & Administrative Officer
*   **Kevin Meeks:** Field President, Chief Revenue & Customer Officer
*   **Christopher Mina:** Chief Technology & Product Officer

LivePerson's Board of Directors includes Jim Miller (Board Chair), Dan Fletcher, Vanessa Pegueros, John Sabino, Karin-Joyce Tjon, William G. Wesemann, Tony Zingale, Ryan L. Vardeman, and Tripp Lane.
