# gemini response

ARHAUS INC (ARHS) is a lifestyle brand and premium retailer in the home furnishings market. The company offers a wide range of products, including furniture for the bedroom, dining room, living room, and home office, as well as outdoor furniture. They also sell lighting, textiles, décor, and bath products like vanities and towels.

Arhaus operates through an omni-channel model, which includes showrooms, an e-commerce platform, catalogs, and in-home designer services. As of December 31, 2021, they had 71 traditional showrooms, 5 Design Studios, and 3 Outlets. The company was founded in 1986 and is headquartered in Boston Heights, Ohio. Arhaus focuses on sustainability and artisanship, with a portion of their products incorporating recycled materials.
