What does LOCKHEED MARTIN CORP (LMT) do?

# gemini response

Lockheed Martin Corporation (LMT) is a global aerospace and defense company that researches, designs, develops, manufactures, integrates, and sustains technology systems, products, and services. The company operates in four main segments: Aeronautics, Missiles and Fire Control (MFC), Rotary and Mission Systems (RMS), and Space.

Lockheed Martin's **Aeronautics** division focuses on combat and air mobility aircraft, as well as unmanned aerial vehicles and related technologies. This segment accounts for approximately $30.3 billion in sales, including tactical aircraft, airlift, sustainment, and aeronautical research and development.

The **Missiles and Fire Control** segment provides air and missile defense systems, tactical missiles, precision strike weapon systems, fire control systems, and ground vehicles. This division contributes about $14.4 billion in sales for integrated air and missile defense, strike, precision fires, air superiority, and close combat weapon systems.

The **Rotary and Mission Systems** segment offers military and commercial helicopters, surface ships, sea and land-based missile defense systems, radar and laser systems, and command and control mission solutions. This segment generates roughly $17.3 billion in sales, encompassing Sikorsky helicopters, maritime systems, sensors, radar systems, command and control, simulation and training, undersea systems, and cyber capabilities.

The **Space** segment provides satellites, space transportation systems, strategic and defensive systems, and classified systems for national security. This segment has sales of approximately $13 billion, focusing on national security, civil, commercial, and weather spacecraft, as well as strategic and missile defense solutions and hypersonics missions.

Lockheed Martin primarily serves the U.S. government and international customers. The company is a major contractor for the U.S. Department of Defense and other federal agencies. They are involved in areas such as hypersonic weapons, cybersecurity, and space exploration.
