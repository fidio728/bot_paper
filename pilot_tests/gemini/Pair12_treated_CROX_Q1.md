What does CROCS INC (CROX) do?

---

# gemini response

Crocs, Inc. designs, develops, manufactures, and distributes casual footwear and accessories for men, women, and children worldwide. The company is known for its foam clogs under the Crocs brand and also owns the HEYDUDE brand of footwear.

Founded in 2002, Crocs has grown to become one of the top ten non-athletic footwear brands globally, with sales exceeding $4 billion and over 150 million pairs of shoes sold in 2025. Approximately 60% of its sales come from North America. The company operates through both wholesale and direct-to-consumer channels, with each contributing about half of its sales.

In addition to its core footwear, Crocs also sells accessories like Jibbitz, which are decorative charms that can be attached to the shoes. The company has also expanded into apparel and has explored other market segments, including golf shoes.
