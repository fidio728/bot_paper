What does ENTRADA THERAPUTIS INC (TRDA) do?

---

# gemini response

Entrada Therapeutics, Inc. (TRDA) is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on developing a new class of medicines that can reach intracellular targets previously considered undruggable. They aim to treat devastating diseases by creating "intracellular biologics".

The company utilizes a proprietary platform called the Endosomal Escape Vehicle (EEV™) to enable the efficient delivery of biologics into cells. This technology allows for the development of therapies for conditions such as neuromuscular diseases (including Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy) and inherited retinal diseases. Entrada Therapeutics is also exploring applications in immunology and oncology. Their pipeline includes preclinical candidates and a clinical-stage program for myotonic dystrophy type 1.
