# you asked

What does PINTEREST INC (PINS) do?

---

# gemini response

Pinterest, Inc. (PINS) is an American social media service that operates as a visual discovery engine and platform for sharing and saving ideas. Often described as a "visual search engine" or a "virtual dream board," Pinterest allows users to discover, collect, and organize inspiration on a wide range of topics, including recipes, home decor, fashion, DIY projects, and travel.

The platform's core components are "Pins," which are images or videos linked to external websites, and "Boards," which are collections where users save Pins based on themes or projects. Users can "pin" content from across the web using browser extensions or the "Save" button, and can also save Pins created by other users through a process called "repinning".

While it has social media aspects, Pinterest is distinct from other platforms as users are often actively looking to plan purchases or find solutions to specific needs, rather than primarily engaging in social interaction. This intent-driven user behavior makes it a valuable platform for businesses seeking to build brand awareness, showcase products, and drive sales. Businesses can create "Promoted Pins" to increase their reach and connect with audiences interested in their content and products. Pinterest's AI-powered "Taste Graph" helps match businesses with users who are most likely to be interested in their offerings.
