What does PATTERN GROUP INC (PTRN) do?

---

# gemini response

PATTERN GROUP INC (PTRN) is a global e-commerce accelerator that helps brands expand their sales across online marketplaces worldwide. The company buys inventory directly from its brand partners and resells these products to consumers on over 60 marketplaces globally, including Amazon, Walmart, and Target.

At the core of Pattern's operations is its proprietary Ecommerce Acceleration Platform (EXP), which utilizes artificial intelligence and machine learning to process vast amounts of data. This platform analyzes over 46 trillion data points, with billions more added weekly, to optimize various aspects of e-commerce, such as pricing, advertising, content, forecasting, and customer service. By leveraging this data and technology, Pattern aims to "unlock" demand and growth for its brand partners.

Pattern acts as a full-stack partner, managing inventory, pricing, logistics, and analytics with end-to-end control. This approach allows brands to scale their online sales without needing to build large in-house teams. The company's business model combines distribution and service-based income, generating revenue through retail margins on resold products, fees for its analytics and logistics platform, and revenue from managing ad campaigns. Pattern also helps brands maintain pricing integrity and protect their brand equity in a marketplace where counterfeit sellers are common.

Pattern Group has experienced significant revenue growth, with reported revenues of $1.14 billion in the first half of 2025, a 35% increase year-over-year. The company operates in over 70 marketplaces globally, with a majority of its revenue historically coming from Amazon, though non-Amazon channels have shown strong recent growth.
