What does LIVEPERSON INC (LPSN) do?

# gemini response

LivePerson Inc. (LPSN) is a software publisher focused on conversational artificial intelligence and digital customer engagement. The company's core offering is its Conversational Cloud platform, which enables businesses to connect with consumers at scale through messaging and other digital channels. LivePerson's technology is used by major brands to modernize contact centers and run digital customer conversations.

The Conversational Cloud is an open, API-first platform that integrates with existing Customer Relationship Management (CRM) and Contact Center as a Service (CCaaS) systems. This allows businesses to manage customer interactions across various channels, treating the ongoing dialogue as the primary unit of engagement. LivePerson's platform powers nearly a billion conversational interactions monthly, providing analytics and safety tools to help businesses govern AI usage and monitor performance.

The company also emphasizes AI governance and safety features, offering tools to monitor AI and agent performance, detect AI drift, and support compliance with emerging frameworks like the EU AI Act. LivePerson serves industries such as financial services, retail, travel and hospitality, healthcare, and telecommunications.
