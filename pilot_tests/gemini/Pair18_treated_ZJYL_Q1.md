# gemini response

JIN MEDICAL INTERNATIONAL LTD (ZJYL) is a holding company that designs, develops, manufactures, and sells rehabilitation equipment. Their primary products include wheelchairs and wheelchair components, which are their key revenue generators. The company also offers other living aid products for individuals with disabilities or impaired mobility, such as oxygen concentrators, bathing machines, respiratory and walking aids, and beauty instruments.

Jin Medical International distributes its products both domestically in China and internationally, with significant sales in Japan and China. The company operates manufacturing plants in China and is establishing a new facility in Chuzhou, Anhui Province. Founded in 2006, the company is headquartered in Changzhou, China.
