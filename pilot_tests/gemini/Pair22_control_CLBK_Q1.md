The company's core operations include:
*   **Lending:** Offering commercial loans (including multi-family and commercial real estate, business loans, and construction loans), residential loans (such as one-to-four family residential real estate), and consumer loans (like home equity loans, auto loans, and personal loans).

*   **Ancillary Financial Services:** Offering wealth management, financial planning, asset management, and title insurance products.
*   **Cash Management Services:** Including remote deposit, lockbox services, sweep accounts, and escrow services.
