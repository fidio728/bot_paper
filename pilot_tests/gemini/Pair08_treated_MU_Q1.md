What does MICRON TECHNOLOGY INC (MU) do?

---

# gemini response

Micron Technology, Inc. (MU) is a multinational semiconductor company that designs, manufactures, and sells memory and storage products worldwide. They are a global leader in innovative memory solutions, producing DRAM (dynamic random-access memory), NAND flash memory, and SSDs (solid-state drives). These products are crucial for a wide range of applications, including AI, cloud computing, automotive, mobile devices, and personal computers.

Micron is the only major American computer memory manufacturer and is considered one of the "Big Three" globally, alongside Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix. The company markets its consumer products under the brand name Crucial. Micron is currently making significant investments in expanding its manufacturing facilities, particularly in the United States. The company's products are essential for enabling data-driven innovation across various industries. Micron is also experiencing growth due to high investments in AI infrastructure, particularly in High-Bandwidth Memory (HBM).
