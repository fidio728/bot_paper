Acadian Timber engages in the entire lifecycle of forest products, from planning and growing trees to harvesting, marketing, and sales. The company emphasizes sustainable forestry practices, including reforestation and selective harvesting, to maintain the ecological health of its land.

In addition to traditional timber operations, Acadian Timber also participates in the voluntary carbon credit market, further demonstrating its commitment to responsible land management. Acadian Timber is also known for being a consistent dividend payer.
