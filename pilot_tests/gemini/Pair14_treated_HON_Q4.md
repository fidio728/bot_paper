HONEYWELL INTERNATIONAL INC (HON) management team and leadership

# gemini response

Vimal Kapur serves as the Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of Honeywell International Inc. He assumed the CEO role in June 2023 and became Chairman in 2024, bringing nearly four decades of experience in automation and global industries. Kapur has focused on accelerating growth and transforming the company's portfolio, including approximately $14 billion in acquisitions. He was instrumental in Honeywell's separation into three public companies: Honeywell Aerospace, Solstice Advanced Materials, and Honeywell.

The leadership team at Honeywell is responsible for the company's strategic direction and execution across its business sectors, which include aerospace, building automation, industrial automation, and energy and sustainability solutions. The average tenure of the management team is 2.7 years.
