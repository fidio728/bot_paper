# gemini response

AnaptysBio, Inc. (ANAB) is a clinical-stage biotechnology company focused on discovering and developing therapeutic antibody candidates for unmet medical needs in inflammation and autoimmune diseases. The company utilizes a proprietary somatic hypermutation platform to create and refine human antibodies with improved efficacy and safety profiles, aiming to accelerate target validation and candidate selection for various immune-mediated conditions.

AnaptysBio's pipeline includes several clinical-stage programs targeting dermatological and inflammatory disorders. The company is also undergoing a business separation, planning to spin off its biopharma operations into a new public company called First Tracks Biotherapeutics in Q2 2026. This separation aims to unlock value by creating two distinct companies: one focused on biopharma and the other managing royalty assets from collaborations, which will initially retain the AnaptysBio name and ticker symbol (ANAB).
