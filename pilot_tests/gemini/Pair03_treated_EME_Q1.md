# gemini response

EMCOR Group, Inc. (NYSE: EME) is a Fortune 500 company that provides a range of services in mechanical and electrical construction, energy infrastructure, and building services. The company operates through numerous subsidiaries and serves a diverse client base across commercial, technology, manufacturing, industrial, healthcare, utility, and institutional sectors. EMCOR's services are focused on building, powering, servicing, and protecting facilities and critical infrastructure.

Their offerings include:
*   **Mechanical and Electrical Construction:** This segment involves constructing and operating infrastructure such as power stations, including sustainable energy systems like photovoltaic systems, as well as food processing facilities, road lighting, and traffic control systems.
*   **Industrial and Energy Infrastructure Services:** EMCOR works on projects related to energy infrastructure, including those utilizing sustainable energy and carbon capture technologies. This also includes the maintenance of oil refineries and petrochemical processing plants.
*   **Building Services:** This encompasses the ongoing operation and servicing of facilities, including mechanical and HVAC equipment, electrical and lighting infrastructure, security, and fire protection systems.

EMCOR Group's clients include data centers, oil refineries, semiconductor, biotech, life sciences, and pharmaceutical facilities, as well as various government entities. The company has a significant presence in the United States, with some operations in the United Kingdom as well.
