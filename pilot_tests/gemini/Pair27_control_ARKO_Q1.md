What does ARKO CORP (ARKO) do?

# gemini response

ARKO Corp. (ARKO) is a Fortune 500 company that operates as a holding company for GPM Investments, LLC. It is one of the largest operators of convenience stores and wholesalers of fuel in the United States.

The company's operations are divided into four segments:
*   **Retail:** This segment includes convenience stores that sell fuel and merchandise to consumers. These stores offer prepared foods, beverages, snacks, and are partnered with quick-serve restaurant brands.
*   **Wholesale:** ARKO supplies fuel to third-party dealers and consignment agents through this segment.
*   **GPM Petroleum:** This segment is responsible for supplying fuel to ARKO's retail and wholesale sites, as well as distributing fuel.
*   **Fleet Fueling:** This segment operates cardlock locations, manages third-party fueling sites, and markets fuel cards for commercial customers.

ARKO Corp. is based in Richmond, Virginia, and its common stock trades on the Nasdaq under the symbol ARKO. The company also offers a loyalty program called fas REWARDS®, which provides savings on merchandise and gas.
