# you asked

What does AI ERA CORP (AERA) do?

---

# gemini response

AI Era Corp. (AERA) is an intellectual property (IP) firm that focuses on acquiring, developing, and licensing various forms of IP, primarily within the entertainment and media sectors. The company leverages artificial intelligence to create what it calls "Agentic AI-powered media ecosystems".

Key aspects of AI Era Corp.'s business include:

*   **Intellectual Property (IP) Focus:** AI Era Corp. acquires and licenses IP related to films, TV series, and short-form dramas. They own IP for UFilm.ai (a generative AI production platform), Uflix.ai (a distribution platform), and the NFT Movie Marketplace (MMM).
*   **AI-Powered Content Creation and Distribution:** The company uses AI technology to generate content and provide tools for content creation, aiming to place "studio-grade storytelling tools in the hands of anyone with a smartphone". They aim to integrate UFilm.ai (generative AI) with Uflix.ai (distribution) to create an AI streaming ecosystem.
*   **Film and Media Operations:** AI Era Corp. also operates a physical movie theater and distribution hub in Mount Kisco, New York. This operation serves as a link between traditional cinema and their AI-driven media ventures.
*   **Monetization:** Revenue streams include direct sales of copyrights, licensing agreements, embedded marketing services, consulting on AI solutions, and operations of their cinema. They also aim to monetize AI assets through their NFT marketplace and AI Agent SaaS offerings.

Recently, AI Era Corp. appointed Ahmad Moradi as its new CEO to lead the commercialization and expansion of its AI-driven creator ecosystem. The company has also secured strategic distribution deals to deliver its AI-generated content to global audiences.
