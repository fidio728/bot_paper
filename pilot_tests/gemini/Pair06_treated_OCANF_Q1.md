# gemini response

OceanaGold Corporation (OCANF) is an intermediate gold and copper producer engaged in the exploration, development, and operation of mineral properties. The company is headquartered in Vancouver, British Columbia, and has a portfolio of four operating mines: the Haile Gold Mine in the United States, the Didipio Mine in the Philippines, and the Macraes and Waihi operations in New Zealand.

OceanaGold produces gold and silver as dore bars, and copper in concentrate. Their gold and copper are utilized in sectors such as renewable energy, transportation, medical devices, and technology. The company focuses on safely and responsibly maximizing free cash flow from its operations and delivering returns to shareholders.

In 2024, OceanaGold achieved record financial results, including an annual revenue of $1.29 billion, a net profit of $192 million, and free cash flow of $245 million. The company also grew its total Mineral Reserves by 27% to 6.2 million ounces of gold.
