What does AMKOR TECHNOLOGY INC (AMKR) do?

---

# gemini response

Amkor Technology, Inc. (AMKR) is a global leader in outsourced semiconductor packaging and test services. They provide a comprehensive range of solutions, including advanced packaging, wafer-level processing, and system-in-package services. Amkor designs, packages, and tests semiconductors for other companies, essentially acting as a subcontractor in the semiconductor supply chain.

Their services are crucial for bringing advanced technologies to market, supporting applications in areas such as smartphones, data centers, artificial intelligence, automobiles, and wearables. Amkor's offerings include wafer bump, package design, back-grind, system-level testing, and advanced packaging solutions like flip-chip, wirebond, MEMS, and fan-out. They also develop and offer various package formats and technologies. The company has a broad geographic footprint with factories in several countries.

Amkor is a significant player in the semiconductor industry, and its strategic partnerships, particularly with companies like Nvidia, position it for future growth in advanced semiconductor packaging. The company is also investing in expanding its capabilities, including a major facility in Arizona supported by US Chips Act funding, which is set to begin advanced packaging for Nvidia in the coming years.
