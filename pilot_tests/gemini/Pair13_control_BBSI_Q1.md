What does BARRETT BUSINESS SVCS INC (BBSI) do?

---

# gemini response

Barrett Business Services, Inc. (BBSI) is a business management solutions company that offers human resource outsourcing and professional management consulting services. They primarily serve small to medium-sized businesses across 29 states in the US, with a significant client base that spans all 50 states.

BBSI's services are structured around a Professional Employer Organization (PEO) model, where they establish a co-employment relationship with their clients. This allows BBSI to handle various business functions, including:

*   **Payroll Processing:** Managing payroll, tax filings, and ensuring compliance with wage and hour laws.
*   **Human Resources:** Providing expertise in employee relations, policy development, hiring, onboarding, performance management, and terminations.
*   **Workers' Compensation:** Offering coverage, managing claims directly, and implementing safety programs to reduce workplace injuries and claims. BBSI is also a self-insured Workers' Comp carrier.
*   **Employee Benefits:** Coordinating and administering benefits such as medical, dental, vision, and 401(k) plans.
*   **Risk and Safety Management:** Developing safety programs, providing training, and ensuring OSHA compliance.
*   **Staffing and Recruiting:** Assisting businesses in finding and hiring the right employees.
*   **Business Strategy and Consulting:** Partnering with clients to analyze operations, set goals, and implement strategies for growth and efficiency.

By outsourcing these functions, BBSI aims to help business owners save time, reduce administrative burdens, and focus on core revenue-generating activities.
