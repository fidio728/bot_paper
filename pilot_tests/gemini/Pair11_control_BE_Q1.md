What does BLOOM ENERGY CORP (BE) do?

---

# gemini response

Bloom Energy Corp. (BE) designs, manufactures, sells, and installs solid oxide fuel cell (SOFC) systems for on-site electricity generation. Their technology converts fuels like natural gas, biogas, or hydrogen into electricity through an electrochemical process, rather than combustion.

The company offers its "Bloom Energy Server" platform, which provides a compact and modular solution for power generation. These systems can be installed quickly, in as little as 90 days, and can scale incrementally to meet energy demands. Bloom Energy's solutions are known for being resilient, reliable, and offering high uptime, which is particularly crucial for industries like data centers.

Bloom Energy also provides "Bloom Electrolyzer" for hydrogen production. Their products are used by various industries, including utilities, data centers, retail, healthcare, education, and manufacturing, aiming to deliver lower-carbon energy solutions. The company's technology is seen as a way to address the increasing power demands of infrastructure, such as AI data centers, which traditional grids may struggle to meet.
