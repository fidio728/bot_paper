What does GARRETT MOTION INC (GTX) do?

---

# gemini response

Garrett Motion Inc. (GTX) is a global technology company that designs, manufactures, and sells engineered turbocharging and electric-boosting technologies. They specialize in products for various powertrains, including gasoline, diesel, natural gas, and electrified systems, for both light and commercial vehicles.

The company's offerings extend to air and fluid compression technologies, as well as high-speed electric motors and connected vehicle technologies. Garrett Motion also develops zero-emission technologies, such as fuel cell compressors for hydrogen vehicles and thermal management systems for battery electric vehicles.

In addition to serving original equipment manufacturers (OEMs), Garrett Motion also sells its technologies in the aftermarket through a distribution network. The company has a long history, originating as part of Garrett AiResearch's Industrial Division in 1954, and was formerly known as Honeywell Transportation Systems.
