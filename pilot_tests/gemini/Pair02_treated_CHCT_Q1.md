# you asked

What does COMMUNITY HEALTHCARE TRUST (CHCT) do?

---

# gemini response

Community Healthcare Trust (CHCT) is a real estate investment trust (REIT) that focuses on acquiring, owning, and leasing income-producing healthcare properties, primarily in non-urban markets across the United States. These properties are leased to hospitals, doctors, healthcare systems, and other healthcare service providers.

The company's strategy involves acquiring essential, often smaller, healthcare facilities, sometimes through off-market deals, and securing long-term leases with healthcare providers to ensure stable and predictable cash flow. CHCT's portfolio includes properties such as physician offices, specialty centers, behavioral facilities, inpatient rehabilitation facilities, and medical office buildings. As of December 31, 2025, the company had investments in approximately 198 real estate properties valued at around $1.2 billion. CHCT operates as a self-managed REIT and generates revenue primarily through rental income from these properties under long-term agreements.
