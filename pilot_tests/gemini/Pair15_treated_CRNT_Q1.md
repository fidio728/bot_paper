# you asked

What does CERAGON NETWORKS LTD (CRNT) do?

---

# gemini response

Ceragon Networks Ltd. is a telecommunications equipment company that specializes in providing end-to-end wireless connectivity solutions. They design and manufacture high-capacity communication systems for wireless backhaul, mid-haul, and front-haul, which are crucial for connecting cell sites to a network's core.

The company's products utilize microwave and millimeter wave radio technology to transfer telecommunication traffic between base stations, small cells, and service provider networks. Ceragon's solutions support 5G and 4G broadband wireless connectivity, as well as mission-critical services that require high reliability and speed. Their offerings include hardware, software, and AI-powered managed and professional services.

Ceragon serves a diverse customer base, including mobile network operators, wireless service providers, private network operators, utilities, public safety organizations, and government agencies. They have a global presence with sales offices and partnerships worldwide, and their solutions are deployed in over 130 countries. The company has also made strategic acquisitions, such as Siklu, to enhance its millimeter wave capabilities, and End 2 End Technologies, to strengthen its presence in private networks.
