GLOBAL MOFY AI LTD (GMM) is a technology solutions provider that specializes in virtual content production and the development of digital assets. The company utilizes its proprietary "Mofy Lab" technology platform, which combines 3D reconstruction and artificial intelligence (AI) interactive technologies, to create high-definition virtual versions of various physical objects, characters, and scenes.

These digital assets can be used in a wide range of applications, including movies, TV series, AR/VR, animation, advertising, and gaming. Global Mofy AI also monetizes AI-generated content and offers services such as visual effect design, content development, production, integration, technology development, and software development. Previously known as Global Mofy Metaverse Limited, the company changed its name to Global Mofy AI Limited in August 2024.
