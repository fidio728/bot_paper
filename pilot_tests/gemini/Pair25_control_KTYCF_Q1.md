What does KITS EYECARE LTD (KTYCF) do?

---

# gemini response

KITS EYECARE LTD (KTYCF) operates as a vertically integrated, digitally native eyecare platform. The company provides a wide selection of contact lenses and eyeglasses, including its own KITS-designed products, along with online vision tools. KITS EYECARE LTD sells its products through a network of e-commerce websites such as KITS.com and KITS.ca.

The company offers a range of products including progressive, polarized, and blue light blocking lenses, as well as frames, accessories, and sunglasses for men and women. KITS EYECARE LTD partners with various brands for its contact lenses, including Alcon, Bausch & Lomb, CooperVision, and Johnson & Johnson, and for its glasses, it partners with brands like Gucci, Oakley, Ray-Ban, and Tom Ford.

KITS EYECARE LTD's business model focuses on removing intermediaries in the traditional optical industry through its technology and manufacturing processes, allowing it to offer a wide selection at competitive prices. The company primarily operates in the United States and Canada. While the company started with a focus on contact lenses (which still represent the majority of its revenue), it has expanded into eyeglasses and is also exploring physical showrooms and developing smart glasses with AI.
