LEON'S FURNITURE LTD (LEFUF) is a Canadian retailer that sells home furnishings, mattresses, appliances, and electronics. The company operates under various banners, including Leon's, The Brick, Brick Outlet, The Brick Mattress Store, Midnorthern Appliance, and Appliance Canada. In addition to retail sales, LEFUF also provides repair services for furniture, electronics, and appliances through its division, Trans Global Services (TGS).

The company also offers warranties and credit insurance products. LEFUF operates through a network of approximately 298 retail stores across Canada and maintains an online presence with six e-commerce websites. Founded in 1909, the company is headquartered in Toronto, Canada.
