What does TRONOX HOLDINGS PLC (TROX) do?

# gemini response

Tronox Holdings PLC (TROX) is a global leader in the production of titanium dioxide (TiO2) pigment, a key ingredient that provides whiteness and opacity to paints, coatings, plastics, and paper. The company operates a vertically integrated "mine-to-pigment" business model, meaning it controls the entire process from extracting titanium-bearing mineral sands to manufacturing the final TiO2 product.

In addition to TiO2 pigment, Tronox also produces specialty-grade titanium dioxide products, high-purity titanium chemicals (such as titanium tetrachloride), and zircon. These products have a wide range of applications, including in inks, fibers, rubber, food, cosmetics, and pharmaceuticals. The company mines its own titanium-bearing mineral sands, achieving a high degree of ore self-sufficiency, which helps buffer it against raw material price fluctuations.

Tronox has mining operations in Australia and South Africa, and pigment production facilities in the United States, the Netherlands, and Western Australia. While the company is a significant player in the global chemical and mining industries, it is also undergoing a transformation, with an increasing focus on the rare earth minerals market.
