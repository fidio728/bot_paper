Sally Beauty Holdings, Inc. (SBH) is an international specialty retailer and distributor of professional beauty supplies. The company operates through two main segments: Sally Beauty Supply and Beauty Systems Group (BSG).

Sally Beauty Supply caters to both retail consumers and salon professionals, offering a wide array of beauty products including hair care and color, skin and nail care items, and styling tools. They carry both third-party brands and exclusive-label products.

Beauty Systems Group (BSG) focuses on professional salon owners and stylists, providing them with professional beauty products through specialized stores, e-commerce platforms, and a sales force. This segment offers a range of products including professional hair color, salon equipment, and styling tools, often under exclusive brands for salon use and resale.

Founded in 1964, Sally Beauty Holdings operates a significant number of stores worldwide, including company-operated and franchised locations.
