What does HONEYWELL INTERNATIONAL INC (HON) do?

# gemini response

Honeywell International Inc. is a multinational conglomerate corporation that operates in diverse sectors, including aerospace, building automation, industrial automation, and energy and sustainability solutions. The company offers a wide range of products and services, from avionics and aircraft engines to control systems for buildings and solutions for refining and petrochemical processes.

Honeywell's core business segments are:
*   **Aerospace Technologies:** This segment provides avionics, aircraft engines, flight management systems, and services for aircraft manufacturers, airlines, military, and space programs.
*   **Building Automation:** This segment offers products and software for building control and optimization, including sensors, security systems, and fire products for commercial buildings.
*   **Industrial Automation:** This segment focuses on automation control, instrumentation, and sensing technologies to optimize manufacturing and distribution operations. It also provides productivity solutions and services.
*   **Performance Materials and Technologies (PMT) / Energy and Sustainability Solutions:** This segment includes businesses like Honeywell UOP, which delivers process technology, equipment, and services for refining, petrochemicals, and low-carbon energy. This segment also encompasses advanced materials and specialty chemicals.

Historically, Honeywell has been involved in various industries, including early advancements in heating controls, a period in the computer industry, and a significant expansion into aerospace. The company has grown through numerous acquisitions and mergers, notably with AlliedSignal in 1999. As of late 2025, Honeywell is undergoing a strategic realignment, planning to separate into three independent companies focused on automation, aerospace, and advanced materials.
