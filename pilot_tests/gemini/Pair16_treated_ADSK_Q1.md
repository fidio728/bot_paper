What does AUTODESK INC (ADSK) do?

# gemini response

Autodesk, Inc. (ADSK) is a multinational software corporation that specializes in providing 3D design, engineering, and entertainment technology solutions. The company's software is used across various industries, including architecture, engineering, construction, manufacturing, media, and entertainment.

Autodesk's product offerings cater to specific industry needs:
*   **Architecture, Engineering, and Construction (AEC):** Autodesk provides tools for building information modeling (BIM), civil infrastructure design, and construction management. Key products include AutoCAD, Revit, and Autodesk Build.
*   **Product Design and Manufacturing (PDM):** This segment offers solutions for 3D mechanical design, simulation, and production. Products like Fusion 360 and Inventor are used in this area.
*   **Media and Entertainment (M&E):** Autodesk delivers software for visual effects, animation, color grading, and game development. Popular software in this category includes Maya and 3ds Max.

Founded in 1982, Autodesk's flagship software, AutoCAD, revolutionized computer-aided design (CAD) by making it widely accessible. The company's software has been used in numerous high-profile projects, from skyscrapers to films and even in the development of electric cars. Autodesk also focuses on harnessing emerging technologies like AI, 3D printing, and robotics to enhance its software and services, aiming to help companies and individuals work more efficiently and sustainably.
