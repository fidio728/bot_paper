# gemini response

Ero Copper Corp. (ERO) is a mining company primarily focused on the exploration, development, and production of copper and its byproducts, such as gold and silver, in Brazil. The company's main operations are in Brazil, including the Caraíba Operations in Bahia State, the Tucuma Operation in Pará State, and the Xavantina Operations in Mato Grosso State.

Ero Copper Corp. also holds interests in additional copper projects in Pará and Mato Grosso states. The company operates through its subsidiaries, including Mineracao Caraiba S.A. (MCSA), which owns the Caraiba Operations, and NX Gold S.A., which owns the Xavantina Operations. Ero Copper Corp. was founded in 2016 and is headquartered in Vancouver, Canada.
