What does MACY'S INC (M) do?

---

# gemini response

Macy's, Inc. (M) is a major American holding company that operates department stores and beauty stores. Its primary brands include Macy's and Bloomingdale's, along with the beauty store chain Bluemercury.

The company offers a wide array of merchandise, including clothing, footwear, accessories, cosmetics, home furnishings, jewelry, and various consumer goods, for men, women, and children. Macy's operates through a network of physical stores and an e-commerce website, macys.com, providing an omnichannel shopping experience. The company is known for its exclusive brands and collaborations with designers.

Macy's also generates revenue through its credit card programs, managed by FDS Bank. The company has a long history, with the Macy's department store chain founded in 1858. Macy's, Inc. itself was established in 1929 as Federated Department Stores and later renamed Macy's, Inc. in 2007. It is recognized for iconic events like the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade. As of 2023, Macy's is the largest department store company by retail sales in the United States.
