# gemini response

**Dollar Tree, Inc. (DLTR)** is a leading North American discount variety retailer that offers a wide range of consumer products at fixed price points, with many items traditionally priced at $1 or less, and in some sections, up to $5. The company operates under the brands Dollar Tree and Dollar Tree Canada, with a vast network of over 9,000 stores and 18 distribution centers across the contiguous United States and Canada.

Dollar Tree's product selection spans various categories, including:
*   Household essentials and cleaning supplies
*   Snacks, food, and pantry staples
*   Health and beauty products
*   Party supplies and seasonal decorations
*   Toys and crafts
*   Office and school supplies
*   Housewares, glassware, and dinnerware
*   Pet supplies
*   Books

The company's business model emphasizes providing great value and a unique "thrill-of-the-hunt" discovery shopping experience for its customers. Dollar Tree also focuses on efficient supply chains and distribution systems to maintain its low-price strategy. The company was founded in 1986 and has since grown into a Fortune 500 company, with its corporate headquarters located in Chesapeake, Virginia.
