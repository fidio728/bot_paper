(ODFL) is a leading North American less-than-truckload (LTL) motor carrier, providing regional, inter-regional, and national LTL services through a single integrated, union-free organization. Founded in 1934 by Earl Sr. and Lillian Congdon in Richmond, Virginia, the company has grown significantly over the decades. - **LTL Freight Shipping**: Transporting smaller freight loads that do not require a full truck, offering cost-effective solutions for businesses.

- **Expedited Transportation**: Providing time-sensitive delivery options to meet urgent shipping needs.

- **Value-Added Services**: Offering container drayage, truckload brokerage, and supply chain consulting to enhance logistics efficiency.
