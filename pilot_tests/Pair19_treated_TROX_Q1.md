- **Mining and Processing:** Tronox operates mineral sand mines in Australia, South Africa, and Brazil, extracting titanium ore, zircon, and other minerals. - **Titanium Dioxide Pigment Production:** The company manufactures TiO₂ pigment, which imparts brightness and durability to products such as paints, coatings, plastics, and paper. Tronox's TiO₂ pigment is marketed under the TiONA® brand. ([tronox.com](https://www.tronox.com/?utm_source=openai))

- **Specialty Products:** Beyond standard TiO₂ pigment, Tronox produces ultrafine specialty TiO₂ products under the CristalACTiV™ brand, high-purity titanium chemicals like titanium tetrachloride, and zircon.
